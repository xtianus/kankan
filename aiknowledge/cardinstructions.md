# Card instructions (agent sections)

- `kankan/cardsections.py` is the pure parser of a card description: specification (text before the first `agent:` heading candidate), agent sections, problems; `for_seat` selects a seat's sections; `help_text` builds the `Instructions` card. `adapter_types` refuses an adapter named `all` (reserved target).
- `kankan task` returns `desc` without agent sections for `impl`, `review` and `hold_wip`, plus `card_instructions` / `card_instructions_error` for `impl` and `review`; Q&A cards are never parsed.
- Invalid headings are checked in `workflow._check_sections`, first thing in `claim_impl` / `claim_review` (inside the claim's project lock). The report lives in the project-level `<gcd>/kankan/card-sections.json` (`cardsections_feedback`), never in seat states: pending actions (comment, add/remove `Error`) are performed by `drain` one per lock hold after a C1 reread; `sweep` only schedules removals, `drain` confirms them; both never raise. Work launchers call them in `tick` and `periodic_board_check`; the Q&A launcher does not.
- `waiting_reasons` gives `invalid_headings` from the board read alone, independent of the record.
- `Error` ownership is tracked separately by `card-sections.json` and `board-settings.json` (`owned_error` plus a write flag `error_write_started`/`error_confirmed`; `owned_error` alone is only a planned write). A refused card moved into the `kankan` list and invalid there has its kankan-written `Error` handed over (`cardsections_feedback._hand_over_to_settings`): the board-settings record gets the ownership flags, then the sections record is dropped; the board settings remove the label later. Board settings evaluate only cards whose `dateLastActivity` is at least 10 seconds older than the read, so tests age moved cards first.
- The `Instructions` card is kankan-owned: `settings_setup.ensure_instructions_card` creates it at the top of the `kankan` list, rewrites it (C9, `TrelloClient.set_card_desc`) and moves it back to the top when another card has a lower `pos` (C3 within the list); `boardconfig.evaluate` reserves its name under the pseudo-key `instructions` (duplicates get `Error`).
