# Trello

- Trello facts: attachment downloads answer 200 without a redirect; an upload's `fileName` is stable across renames; `mimeType` can be null. Checklists (observed 2026-11-10 on the test board): `checklists=all` on the board read gives a top-level array with `idCard` and item `state` (`complete`/`incomplete`), on `GET /1/lists/{id}/cards` it nests them per card; `POST /1/checklists/{id}/checkItems` accepts `checked=true`; ticking and unticking (`PUT /1/cards/{id}/checkItem/{idCheckItem}` `state`) update the card's `dateLastActivity`; both `DELETE /1/cards/{id}/actions/{idAction}/comments` and `DELETE /1/actions/{id}` delete a comment; responses carry a `Date` header.
- `TrelloClient` never follows redirects automatically (`_OPENER` with a no-redirect handler) and sends the credential with `add_unredirected_header`: a redirected JSON call fails; `download` follows at most 3 redirects itself, with the credential only on the `trello_base_url` origin and never downgrading https to http (`_redirect_auth`). Download URLs are validated and requested on `trello_base_url`.
- kankan acts through its own Trello account (`cfg.kankan_member_id`, the token's owner). `notify.mention` mentions a card's creator, or the board's admins for cards kankan created; `notify.answers` forwards comments by board members other than kankan's account. A person's own token is unsupported (Trello never notifies anyone about their own comments, and their answers would be ignored): `init` warns, `doctor` gives a manual step.
- `GET /1/boards/{id}/cards/closed` paged with `before=` loses cards; see [destroy.md](destroy.md).
- Board-wide comments (observed 2026-11-25 on the test board): `GET /1/boards/{id}/actions?filter=commentCard` returns newest first, `since` takes a date or an action ID and excludes that action, `before=<id>` pages, `limit` is at most 1000 (1001 gives 400). Editing a comment changes the text of its `commentCard` action and deleting it removes the action; neither produces an `updateComment` or `deleteComment` action in that read. The board read accepts the full attachment fields (`card_attachment_fields=id,name,fileName,url,bytes,mimeType,idMember,isUpload,date`); adding an attachment updates the card's `dateLastActivity`. Renaming a card does not notify its watchers (checked by the user).
